# Final Findings

- The dataset contains 8,760 hourly observations covering 2017-12-01 to 2018-11-30.
- Total recorded rentals are 6,172,314, with an average of 704.60 rentals per hour.
- The strongest average-demand hour is 18:00, at approximately 1,502.93 rentals per hour.
- Summer is the strongest season by average hourly demand (1,034.07).
- Temperature has a positive relationship with rental demand, while humidity, rainfall and snowfall have negative relationships.
- Rainy and snowy observations show substantially lower average rental demand than dry observations.
- The dataset has no missing cells and no duplicate rows.

## Exactly 3 Business Recommendations

1. **Increase bike availability before the evening peak:** Rebalance bikes toward high-demand locations ahead of the 17:00–20:00 period, with special focus on the strongest hour identified in the analysis.
2. **Use weather-aware fleet planning:** Reduce unnecessary redistribution during rain/snow and use weather forecasts to adjust staffing, maintenance and bike availability proactively.
3. **Use seasonal demand forecasting:** Allocate more operational capacity during high-demand seasons and reduce excess fleet movement during low-demand winter periods.
